## 2. Procedural 3D Geometries & Shaders

1. `GlassTorusHero`: Torus geometry + inner TorusKnot plasma filament with transmission material.
2. `AuroraRibbon`: CatmullRomCurve3 tube with dynamic vertex oscillation.
3. `ServiceMonoliths`: 6 instanced box slabs + orbital light ring.
4. `HardwareShowcase`: Rounded box chassis + dynamic HTML5 Canvas dashboard texture.
5. `InfinityLoop`: Parametric Lemniscate of Bernoulli tube + 5 glowing node spheres.
6. `ProductMonoliths`: 3 tilted glass slabs + inner undulating silk wave mesh.
7. `ChipProcessorStack`: 3-tiered exploded glass processors + center glowing light.
8. `NeuralBrain`: Dual-hemisphere cortical spheres + temporal knot creases on 3-tiered pedestal.
9. `DesignArtboards`: 3 curved glass viewport planes + vector spline handles.
10. `GlassVantixioTotem`: Monumental beveled 'V' glass geometry on pedestal.
11. `StargateAndEarth`: Colossal 'V' stargate arch + 3D Earth sphere with atmospheric rim.
12. `TerrainEnvironment`: Reflective water plane (`roughness: 0.08`, `metalness: 0.85`), shoreline ring, mountain silhouettes, and star field.
