## SCREEN 06: OUR PRODUCTS (IN-HOUSE PLATFORMS)

### 1. Screen Metadata
- **Screen Number**: 06 / 15
- **Screen Name**: Our Products
- **Main Purpose**: Establish product credibility by demonstrating proprietary in-house platforms developed and operated by Vantixio.
- **Kicker**: `OUR PRODUCTS`
- **Headline**: "Products built for *real-world operations.*"
- **Subtext**: "In-house platforms that solve everyday problems at scale — from laundry and garment care to retail and vendor operations."
- **CTAs & Indicators**:
  - Left CTA: `Explore Products ↗` (Pill button with glowing border)
  - Right pagination: Counter `01 / 03`, horizontal progress track, navigation arrows `(← →)`.
- **Featured Products**:
  1. `YesDhobi` — Laundry & Garment Care Platform (T-shirt icon, circular arrow trigger `↗`)
  2. `Theka` — POS & Retail Management (Bar chart icon)
  3. `Dhobicare` — Operations & Vendor System (Stacked layers icon)

### 2. Deep Section Analysis
- **A. Visual Composition**:
  - Three monumental glass monolith cards arranged in an aggressive 3D perspective receding from right (foreground hero) to left (background).
  - An orbital cyan glass ribbon wraps around the base of the monoliths, visually tying them to the reflective fjord water.
  - Generous negative space in the upper left balances the prominent typography and primary CTA.
- **B. Branding**:
  - Reinforces Vantixio's identity as true product builders who solve complex real-world operational challenges rather than just offering surface consulting.
- **C. 3D Elements**:
  - **Objects**: Three massive, rounded-corner glass slabs with physical thickness and internal refraction.
  - **Interior Art**: The hero `YesDhobi` card contains an internal, undulating silk-like light wave, giving the impression that digital energy is physically encapsulated within the glass.
  - **Materiality**: High-transmission glass with specular cyan edge highlights and Fresnel reflections.
- **D. User Experience**:
  - Intuitive carousel navigation: Visitors can click the right/left arrows or drag horizontally to rotate between `YesDhobi`, `Theka`, and `Dhobicare`.
  - Direct conversion via `Explore Products ↗`.
- **E. Animation Possibilities**:
  - **Transition from 05**: The Infinity Loop from Screen 05 uncoils; its dual loops open up and slice into three vertical glass monoliths that drop or rise into position with inertia.
  - **Card Carousel**: Stepping pagination translates the cards along a 3D Z-axis/X-axis curve (`transform-style: preserve-3d`), moving the next card into the foreground hero position.
  - **Internal Wave**: Continuous gentle vertex distortion inside the glass card shader.
- **F. Technical Interpretation**:
  - Three.js 3D planes with custom transmission shaders or CSS 3D matrix transforms synchronized with GSAP Draggable / Swiper.
